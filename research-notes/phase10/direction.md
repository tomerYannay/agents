# Phase 10 research direction (Research Director, 2026-10-13). This is the final phase.

## What Phases 5–9 established
1. Newcomer success is about 4–6% per attempt in every dataset measured.
2. Followers arrive within about 6 months. Moats a $2k founder can buy don't stop them (Phase 9).
3. Winners without an audience over-index on:
   - high price per account: $300+/month gives 5.1× lift;
   - small teams: 4.4×;
   - paid or partner channels: Meta, TikTok and Google Ads, affiliates and partnerships give 3–6×.

   Launch sites and organic social are near zero.

## Phase 10 direction: high-ticket offers with paid acquisition, whose unit economics survive copying
Target the measured winning traits directly. The offer must:
- be priced at $300+ per account per month, or $1,500+ one-off (or a renewal equivalent);
- be sold through search ads, paid social or partner/affiliate channels;
- have delivery the founder can automate or delegate. Pure agency or freelance hours are excluded unless they are productised with contractors.

For each idea, the agent must give:
- **The paid keyword or audience**, with evidence of advertiser competition. Use public tools only: Google Ads Transparency Center and Meta Ad Library. They may be JavaScript-heavy; never log in or bypass. Cite CPC figures only from published sources.
- **Newcomer outcomes** for comparable offers: TrustMRR, Empire Flippers and other public sources.
- **Unit economics under copying:** what happens to CAC when 3 followers bid on the same keywords.
- **The cheapest real-world test and an explicit kill threshold.** Describe the test only; do not run it.
