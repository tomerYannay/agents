# Phase 10 shortlist (12): high-ticket offers. Claims are as the discovery agents made them (2026-10-13).

Full reports: research-notes/phase10/agent-{A..F}.md. Leader re-check: audit-leaders.md.

## Context: two discovery agents corrected the Phase 8 premise. Reviewers must use this.

**Price.** The "$300+/month gives 5.1× lift" figure counted only profiles that already had revenue.
- Agent D, counting stated prices including pre-revenue profiles:
  - Top plan $300+: 10.3% reach $3k MRR (22/214).
  - Cheapest plan $300+: 11.5% (7/61).
  - $100–299: 6.5%. Under $100: 3.2%.
  - So the lift is about 3×, and about 90% of $300+ launches still miss.
- Agent F:
  - Much of the lift is arithmetic: about 10 customers clears $3k.
  - Measured in customer counts, $300+ does worse (30.8% reach 10 subscriptions).
  - 41% of the $300+ group are services or agencies.
  - The residual lift for solo founders without an audience is about 3.6–5.4× (revenue-only frame, n=33).

**Channel.**
- The paid-ads lift is mostly a low-ticket consumer-app effect. 22 of 26 paid-channel winners are consumer apps under $100 per account.
- At high price per account, LinkedIn, cold email, calls and partners do as well as or better than Google or Meta Ads.
- Business Google Ads declarers: 9.1% reach $3k (n=44; baseline 4.7%), and 50 of 97 young Google Ads declarers have $0.

**Paid-traffic economics.**
- Empire Flippers paid-traffic businesses: median margin 28–42%, against 82–95% for others.
- MAP-bound paid dealers: 5–10% margins.
- About 50% of small search advertisers churn each year (Borrell 2009, dated).

**Unmeasurable.**
- Google Ads Transparency Center can only be searched by advertiser name, not keyword, and hit a CAPTCHA after limited use.
- Meta Ad Library is blocked.
- Keyword-level CPCs are not published; only vertical benchmarks exist (LocaliQ 2026: legal $9.87 per click / $131.63 per lead; business services $5.87 / $93.69).

**Agent F's recommended lane.** A B2B vertical tool at $300+/month, delivered the same way for every account, sold by founder outbound plus one partner channel, with paid search only as support. Agent E's E2 is the same idea as a channel template. Both are treated as templates, not scored ideas.
