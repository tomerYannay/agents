# Phase 7 research direction (Research Director, 2026-10-13)

## What Phase 6 taught us
- Newcomer outcomes are now measured on 11 channels (P5: Apify, Superhive, JetBrains, Wix, WooCommerce, App Store hobby apps, CodeCanyon; P6: Skool, G-Cloud, lead-gen/content sites, seeker-paid job search). Everywhere, channels reward incumbents and operators who bring an audience or references; a newcomer without them gets close to nothing.
- After leader corrections, the board is flat (#2–#12 within 2 points). No idea has evidence that THIS founder can acquire customers. Acquisition, not product, is the binding constraint.
- Phase 6 direct-sale ideas (G-Cloud, backflow, Elterngeld, cemeteries) found real spending but long, reference-driven sales cycles or incumbents already occupying the wedge.

## Phase 7 direction: "borrowed, contracted or inherited distribution"
Find businesses where someone who ALREADY owns the customer relationship distributes the founder's product/service, and where there is public evidence that such arrangements pay small/new suppliers:
- white-label / OEM / embedded supply to companies that sell to the end customer (SaaS vendors, agencies, accountants, banks, telcos, insurers, retailers, franchisors);
- wholesale to retailers/distributors rather than DTC (with newcomer evidence: e.g., new brands' wholesale traction);
- revenue-share with audience owners (creators, associations, communities), measured systematically rather than anecdotally;
- manufacturer's-rep / agency-on-commission models where the founder holds the contracts and delegates;
- buying groups, cooperatives, franchise systems and associations where one deal unlocks many units.
Avoid repeating P6 ideas (see ledger). Keep looking globally, not only Israel.

## Agent assignments
- A Consumer: B2B2C — consumer offers distributed through employers (benefits platforms), insurers, banks/card perks, telcos, schools, clinics, retailers; revenue-share with creators measured with public data.
- B Physical: wholesale to independent retailers and distributors; white-label/private-label production for brands or creators that own audiences; corporate/B2B gifting; measure newcomer wholesale traction.
- C Traditional: franchisors, multi-location operators, buying groups/co-ops (hardware, pharmacy, grocery, dental/vet groups), insurer vendor networks, kibbutz/municipal purchasing — one contract → many units.
- D Software: OEM/white-label/embedded software sold to vendors who own customers (SaaS vendors needing a feature/localisation, agencies, accounting firms deploying to clients, banks/telcos); localisation modules paid by global vendors.
- E Distribution: formal partner economics — distributor line cards, referral agreements with professionals, partner programmes that hand leads to small suppliers, procurement through resellers; measure what partners actually deliver to new suppliers.
- F Contrarian: manufacturer's-rep / commission-agent businesses, reverse licensing (rights to sell an existing product in a channel the owner neglects), AI-agent-mediated buying as a new channel, other 2026 shifts creating borrowed distribution.
