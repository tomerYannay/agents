# Phase 6 research direction (Research Director, 2026-10-13)

## What Phases 1–5 established
- No candidate stands out: cumulative #1–#6 span 4.6 points (noise).
- The top of the board is dominated by low-capital digital products sold through open marketplaces or one incumbent's audience (H7, H6, J7, K8, monday app). Phase 5 showed newcomer outcomes on such marketplaces are near zero, so this cluster is probably still overrated where not yet re-checked.
- No idea anywhere has measured customer-acquisition cost. "Direct" ideas (K13, K6, J1) score on hypotheses.
- Repeated themes: Israel-diaspora/affinity (Phases 3–5), regulation/compliance (Phases 1–2), marketplace digital goods (Phases 3–5).

## Business models never examined
1. Lead generation / pay-per-lead / referral-fee businesses (rank-and-rent, comparison sites, B2B appointment setting sold per result).
2. Owned-audience media: niche newsletters, sites and communities monetised by sponsorship, classifieds, job boards, paid membership.
3. Distribution and wholesale: exclusive import/distribution of foreign niche brands, B2B consumables replenishment.
4. Acquisition of existing cash-flowing small businesses or digital assets above the $2k micro tier (seller financing, earn-outs, revenue-share).
5. Licensing / white-label of proven foreign products into under-served languages and geographies (non-Israel too).
6. Non-English, non-Israel markets (LatAm, Brazil, Poland/CEE, Germany, Turkey, Gulf, India) — barely examined beyond one Spanish idea.

## Phase 6 direction: "Owned and direct distribution"
Search for businesses whose customer acquisition does NOT depend on ranking in an open app/asset marketplace, and where there is measurable evidence that small or new operators acquire customers through the channel (job boards with visible posting counts, newsletters with public subscriber/sponsor data, lead-gen sites with published lead prices, distributor margins, acquisition listings with disclosed P&L).

In parallel, a dedicated leader re-check auditor will test the five leaders whose scores rest on unmeasured channels: H6, monday.com app, H1, K8, K13.

## Agent assignments
- A Consumer/prosumer: consumer businesses with owned or direct acquisition — paid communities/cohort courses/newsletters for specific hobbies or life events, consumer services bought through search intent, and consumer opportunities in non-English, non-Israeli markets.
- B Physical: B2B physical supply — niche professional consumables, exclusive regional distribution of foreign niche brands (into Israel or into other under-served markets), replenishment businesses; with distributor margin evidence.
- C Traditional/offline: lead generation and brokerage in traditional sectors (pay-per-lead, referral fees, rank-and-rent) in Israel and abroad; plus buying an existing small offline/online business with seller financing.
- D Niche software/technical: software or data sold DIRECTLY to buyer populations that are enumerable from public registries/lists (licensed professions, registered facilities), where spending on comparable tools is visible; no marketplace dependence.
- E Distribution-first: channels with measurable newcomer economics — niche job boards, B2B newsletters with sponsorship rate cards, partner/reseller and affiliate programs paying recurring commissions, associations' vendor programs, public procurement frameworks.
- F Contrarian/emerging: acquiring under-monetised digital assets (newsletters, apps, sites, SaaS) on public deal marketplaces with disclosed metrics; licensing/white-labelling proven foreign products into new languages/geographies; 2026 structural shifts creating new direct-sale demand.
