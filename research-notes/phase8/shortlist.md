## Leader items for reviewers to challenge

**H7 Blender add-ons: 56.6 → 55.4** (leader auditor)
- Proven channel 3→2; Distribution 67→61; scores 80 / 58 / 61, penalty 12.
- Superhive newcomers' median is 2.5 sales in their first 3 months.
- 5.6% of 2025–26 paid add-ons reach 100+ sales; supply tripled.

**M1 Sage 50 API: held at 52.0** (auditor's downside case 50.8)
- Census: Sage 50 listings are 0.19× QuickBooks Desktop's overall and 0.12× for vertical SaaS; 0 of 97 vendors in legal, nonprofit, property management and payroll list Sage 50.
- The Sage 300 partner agreement (2022) is restrictive. If the Sage 50 terms match, the penalty would be 13–14.
- New evidence from F: the QuickBooks Desktop niche got three copies in 2026.
- New evidence from D: Conductor is the only such API in the TrustMRR sample, and no 2024–26 accounting or ERP integration reached $1k MRR.

**H6 College-baseball API: held at 52.2.** The CFBD jump came from an API paywall in 2025 and a user surge in 2026.

**H2: Agent B suggests a rescore** (revived discontinued smart-home hardware).
- The MiciMike Google Home Mini board raised $133k.
- Bose SoundTouch cloud shut down in February 2026 and Belkin Wemo in January 2026.

**M2 Licensed data API (44.4): new evidence from D**
- SoldComps (eBay sold-prices API, built by parsing pages) reached $21.6k MRR; monthly revenue rose from $347 in April to $19,984 in September 2026; founder has 0 X followers.

## Cross-agent base rates (for scoring "proven channel" and "first customers")

**Agent D (TrustMRR, 69% random sample of about 11.5k; 2024–26 cohort n=5,965)**
- Hit rates: 7.4% reach $1k+ MRR, 3.9% reach $3k+.

- Revenue per subscription of $300+ gives 5.1× lift.
