# Research Methodology (frozen since Phase 3)

Six independent discovery agents (A Consumer/Prosumer, B Physical/E-commerce, C Traditional/Overlooked, D Niche Software/Digital, E Existing Spending/Proven Demand, F Contrarian/Emerging) -> dedupe -> screen -> shortlist ~12-15 -> competitor/pricing/distribution/financial checks -> independent Evidence Auditor -> independent Red-Team -> scoring -> ~5 finalists -> compare with prior leaders -> phase report.

## Scores (do not change)
- Cash-Flow (100): spending evidence 25, speed to first sale 20, capital/delivery feasibility 20, margin 20, independence from founder labor 15.
- Scalability (100): market depth 15, recurring revenue 20, scalable acquisition 20, operating leverage/delegation 20, defensibility 15, founder fit 10.
- Distribution (100): clarity of customer 20, discoverability 20, proven channel evidence 30, difficulty of first customers 15, growth beyond founder outreach 15.
- Balanced = 0.40*CF + 0.40*Scal + 0.20*Dist. Adjusted = Balanced - Evidence Penalty (0-15; historically 6-14; calibrate consistently).
- Gaps under ~5 points are noise.

## Evidence-quality rules
- Platform-wide marketplace revenue is NOT evidence of new-entrant revenue (Phase 5 lesson). Check recent-entrant outcomes, supply growth, concentration.
- Never invent demand/prices/conversion. Label ASSUMPTION / UNKNOWN. Distinguish revenue, gross, contribution, owner profit. Downside/base/upside scenarios for finalists.
- No customer contact, purchases, deployment, or simulated interviews. Document needed validation for later.
